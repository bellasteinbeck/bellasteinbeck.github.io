# Bella Steinbeck - About Me

<img src="https://github.com/bellasteinbeck/bellasteinbeck.github.io/blob/gh-pages/assets/IMG_1148.jpg" alt="My Profile" style="width:50%; border-radius:50%;">


Hi, I’m Bella. I'm recent Georgia Tech Matser's alum (M.S. Biology) who is interested in how science can be applied create a real impact for people, animals, and the world we share. I’ve gained hands-on experience turning complex biological data into actionable insights, whether that means analyzing experiments, identifying patterns, or communicating results in ways that others can actually use. I see data as an important tool for knowing which questions to ask and finding answers that can truly make a difference.

Outside of work, I like to chase adventure whenever possible. I love hiking, rock climbing, and exploring the outdoors, and I’m hoping to get into alpinism/ climbing up mountains someday soon. When I’m not scaling rocks or trails, you’ll probably find me gaming; it’s my way to relax and unwind while still feeding my curiosity, and allows for some quality time with my cat.

I’m now looking to bring my biology background and analytical skills into projects where science meets real-world impact, using data as a tool to make a positive difference. If you’re part of a company or organization that shares these goals, I’d love to connect and explore how I can contribute to meaningful projects.
